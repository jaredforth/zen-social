# Privacy policy

zen social collects no data.

- It does not read, store or send page content, browsing history or personal information.
- Your settings (which features and sites are on, and the pause length) are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`. Chrome syncs them across your signed-in browsers. They never reach the developer or any third party.
- It makes no network requests, has no analytics, and runs no remote code.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/jaredforth/zen-social/issues.
